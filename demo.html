Blokus puzzles: demo

A short version of the dissertation experiment.

In the full study, participants play a practice game and then nine puzzles against a computer that plays perfectly, spending at least 10 minutes on each puzzle. Every move is recorded and scored against perfect play.

This demo has the practice game and one of the nine puzzles.

You can leave a game at any time. Nothing is saved or sent.
